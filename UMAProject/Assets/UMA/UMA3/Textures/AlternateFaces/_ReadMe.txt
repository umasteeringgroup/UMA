The initial face textures were generated by hand by human artists.
These alternate faces were modified from the human created textures using AI assistance and manual editing.
The MatureFace_1 to MatureFace_3 textures (albedo and normal map) were generated by a script: the lines and the skin
variation are computed on the head mesh and added to the Detailed Face albedo and the base face normal map.
The normal maps of Aged_U, Aged_U2, Scar_M, Adult_M, Scar3_U, Scar4_U, TeenFace_1 and TeenFace_2 were generated by a
script from each face's albedo: lines and blemishes that the base face does not have are added to the base face
normal map as relief.
