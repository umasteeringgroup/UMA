UMA Tools 1.31 - Bake Pose to Rest Pose

Use: select the mesh in Object Mode, then open UMA Tools > Rigging and Weights
and click "Bake Pose to Rest Pose — Preserve Shape Keys".

The operation always creates independent mesh, shape-key, armature and object
data in a new <mesh>_RestPose collection, at the original location. It selects
the new mesh and rig. Hide/show the collection to compare with the original.
Materials and unrelated modifier target assets remain shared.

Every stored key, including Basis, is evaluated through Blender's Armature
modifier. Names, order, relative-key links, slider limits/values, masks, mute
flags, interpolation, absolute-key frames, custom properties, and shape-key
animation are retained. Key actions are copied and driver references to the
source mesh/rig/key data are remapped to the new copies.

The copied mesh and rig capture the current frame. Their object animation,
parenting and constraints are frozen to evaluated transforms; rig bone
constraints/animation are baked into the new rest pose. The original objects
and the original rig's other meshes are never edited.

Validation checks every stored key, individual relative keys at slider
endpoints, a combined blend, absolute-key times and midpoints, and the current
display including drivers. It compares world-space vertices and connectivity,
including the downstream viewport modifier stack. Default tolerance is
0.00001 Blender units (adjustable in Adjust Last Operation). Failure removes
the entire new collection and reports the mismatch. A successful collection
stores the per-check JSON report as the uma_rest_pose_validation property.

Supported: one enabled Armature modifier before other enabled modifiers,
vertex-group weights, Preserve Volume, relative and absolute shape keys.
Bone envelopes, multi-modifier skinning, and segmented deform B-Bones are
rejected explicitly. No partial output is kept after a failed geometry check.

Source: UMAToolsNPanel.py (install as a single-file Blender add-on).
Regression tests: tests/test_rest_pose.py (run inside Blender).
Tests use temporary geometry, restore the original scene/selection, and check
for leaked datablocks. They also test UMA30_Body.001 when it is present.
Results (from project root): Build/Validation/BlenderRestPose/results.json.
